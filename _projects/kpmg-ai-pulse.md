## Context

In early 2026, KPMG Portugal wanted to enter the AI conversation, but it had nothing defined yet: no AI message and no service portfolio. The brief from leadership was simply that the firm wanted to talk about AI. Meanwhile, competitors such as PwC and EY were already very visible in the space.

There was no formal AI go-to-market decision yet, so there was no mandate or plan for AI communications. I saw an opportunity to start building KPMG’s presence before the offering was ready.

## The challenge

The real challenge was to talk credibly about AI without a message or services to promote. Anything too promotional would have been empty, and waiting for a formal offering meant leaving the conversation to competitors for even longer.

## Strategy

1. **Build the audience before the offer.** I proposed a newsletter to my direct manager. My argument was that it would position the brand and prepare the ground, so that when the service portfolio was ready, the audience would already know KPMG was active in AI. I chose a newsletter over one-off formats such as a report, posts or articles because it shows real interest. Subscribers and open rates show how many people actually want to hear from you, not just how many saw a post.
2. **Use what already exists.** Instead of producing original research from scratch, I started from KPMG’s global AI research and adapted it for the Portuguese market, adding a local lens through the perspective of KPMG Portugal’s Head of AI.
3. **Maximize reach.** Each issue was published as a LinkedIn newsletter and also sent by email to KPMG’s database, to reach as many people as possible.

## Execution

I wrote each issue in full, including an editorial signed by the Head of AI, written in his voice and approved by him. Each issue takes a global KPMG report as its starting point and frames it for Portuguese business leaders.

The model worked from the first issue and has stayed consistent across the three issues published so far.

## Results

**Internal proof of demand.** AI Pulse showed leadership that the market was listening and willing to hear from KPMG on AI, at a time when the firm was still deciding how to approach the space. The 17,000 subscribers built on KPMG Portugal’s existing LinkedIn audience.

## Learnings

AI Pulse strengthened my confidence in taking initiative without a formal mandate. Instead of waiting for a go-to-market decision, I built a low-risk case for starting early, showing that the work would create value whatever the final offering turned out to be.
