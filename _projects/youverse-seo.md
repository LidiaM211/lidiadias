## Context

Youverse is a startup that develops identity verification software. When I took over content, the website had very little traffic, and the existing content was a handful of blog articles and LinkedIn posts published sporadically over time, with no strategy connecting them.

## The challenge

Youverse works in a highly technical field. Topics such as liveness detection or anti-spoofing are hard to explain, and the audiences making buying decisions are not always technical. The content had to rank in search, be accurate enough for experts, and be clear enough for business buyers, all without paid support to amplify it.

## Strategy

I built the content strategy around topic clusters, both to build search authority and to guide potential customers through the content funnel, from first questions to product-level interest. I selected clusters based on search volume, relevance to the product, and the questions our personas were asking. This led to four clusters:

- Identity verification
- Authentication
- Liveness and anti-spoofing
- Age estimation

## Execution

**Consistent publishing.** I published two articles a week and wrote all of them myself.

**Accuracy through collaboration.** I worked with the sales and product teams so the content was technically accurate and addressed the questions potential customers were actually asking.

**Quarterly content audits.** Every quarter, I reviewed the content for traffic, search intent and outdated information:

- **Top-performing articles:** I checked whether anything needed updating or could be improved, to protect and build on their rankings.
- **Low-performing articles:** I weighed the pros and cons of updating them, merging them with other articles, or removing them.

## Learnings

This project strengthened my ability to turn technical topics into accessible content. Explaining concepts like liveness detection and anti-spoofing in a way that was accurate for experts and clear for business buyers is what made the content work, both in search and in sales conversations.
